# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: startup founders, technology companies, enterprise innovators, and technical executives evaluating SARIV as a product-engineering partner. Secondary: developers, designers, students, researchers, investors, and potential hires studying SARIV's methodology and craft. They arrive skeptical of agency marketing and are judging engineering/design quality by the site itself, not just the copy.

## Product Purpose

The website *is* the product: SARIV's primary proof of capability. It explains what SARIV builds, demonstrates engineering and design quality through its own construction, and converts qualified visitors into leads — without resorting to aggressive sales tactics. Success looks like a visitor concluding "these people build software differently" within seconds, backed by evidence (case studies, craft, technical transparency) rather than assertion.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful & calm, highly technical & confident (never arrogant), curious & patient, disciplined & reserved (never loud or performative), elegant. Voice: clear, concise, confident, measured — engineers speaking to thoughtful clients, no exaggeration or overpromising.

## Anti-references

Stock-photo clichés (handshakes, staged office smiles), neon/cyberpunk aesthetics, glassmorphism-as-decoration, bounce/spin/elastic motion overshoot, generic SaaS gradient-hero templates, short-lived design trends of any kind. Nothing that reads as "marketing page" rather than "engineered application."

## Design Principles

- Precision — details compound into quality; misalignment gets fixed before shipping.
- Simplicity — the best interface is the quietest one; remove friction, don't add decoration.
- Longevity — optimize for years, not launch day; reject trends for structural integrity.
- Integrity — never promise more than the interface delivers.
- Motion is functional, not entertaining — fade/scale/blur/parallax only, never bounce or elastic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: full keyboard navigation, screen-reader-compatible ARIA, visible focus management, `prefers-reduced-motion` support on every animation (crossfade/instant fallback, not motion removed outright).
